History of Japanese Media

Japanese media has a deep history far predating the creation of Anime. For time purposes however (hi aki!) we'll focus on the history of animation in Japan, otherwise known as, Anime.

To understand the importance of Anime, we have to go back to a darker time period; Let's talk about WWII. Despite it's rather meager size, before the 2nd world war, Japan was a global power with influences across East Asia. Ask anyone born in East-Asia during the early 1900s; Japan wasn't taken very lightly. In fact, many people feared Japan as a country. You may think this is due to their military strategies and to that, I wouldn't say you're wrong. However, their strategies were far less paralyzing to the general public. To the general public, more specifically Korean and Chinese citizens, Japan's fear-factor wasn't their military strength, it was their morality.

Before I speak on what happened, I would like to first give my condolences to the families who lost loved ones from this tragedy. What happened was something that no human should ever have to go through.

Let's move onto what Japan did during WWII. Despite the many conflicts going on internally in the countries of China, Japan, and Korea, each country still held their ground in war. However, Japan used their ties with Germany to gain more political and militant power and breifly overtook parts of Korea and China. During that time, Japanese soldier took the lives of many Korean and Chinese citizens and took many of the opposing soldiers into captivity.

Warning: Graphic Descriptions.

With said soldiers, they proceeded to do live-human experimentation. This involved things like rape, forced pregnancy, and even incest. In one experiment, they gave hostages different diseases, including STDs and did surgery on them without anesthesia to see how to human body reacted to said diseases. They also did experimentation with frostbite to see how long a human can last, along with amputating and reattaching different body parts. These are just a small fraction of the absurd amounts of experimentation that they did on these men, women, and children. This incident is infamously known as Unit 731.

After the bombings that happened in Hiroshima and Nagasaki, marking the end of the second world war, Japan found themselves in a sticky situation. Not only were they on the losing side of the war, but they had also lost a sizeable chunk of their populace to bombs and cancer. So, in an attempt to make the world forget about the heinous war crimes they commited, Japan chose to do a complete rebranding. Instead of being held accountable for their actions, the government decided to do two things: First, give their discoveries from the incident to the US in exchange for "forgiveness" and help burying the evidence. Next, they used Anime to distract people from their past. For that reason, Japan dumped a significant amount of money into glorifying Anime. This worked perfectly for them with the growing popularity of the new Anime Astro Boy. Though it wasn't the first one created, it was the earliest successful anime in recorded history.

From there Anime became more and more popular but still not the global talking point that it is today. That is until a man named Akira Toriyama made a series that would go on to change the course of anime forever. This series is, you guessed it, Dragon Ball. Taking inspiration from the Chinese story "Journey to The West (西遊記)" by Wu Cheng'en. This series then went on to inspire generations of mangaka (manga authors/artists). Most notably, Masashi Kishimoto, Kite Kubo, and Eichiro Oda. These mangaka are famous for their hit series, Naruto, Bleach, and One Piece respectively. These series are known as the Big 3 due to their consistent popularity during the "Golden Age" of the Shonen Jump magazine's publication. As of 2025, these series have gone on to inspire a new generation of anime and sub-communities across the world. Now we live in an age where anime has begun to outshine many western TV shows. With streaming services become more atuned to anime watchers, we even have a McDonalds anime. Only time will tell where anime will go from here. Despite the great things that Anime has taught and brought across the world, it is important to never forget the reason it exists in the first place. Once again, my condolences go out to the families affected. May your loved ones rest in peace.
